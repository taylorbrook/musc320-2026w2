# Setup

This folder holds the week 1 audio check and the course kit. Open it in Max before the first class to confirm that sound comes out of your speakers or headphones.

Open sound-check.maxpat in Max, turn audio on in the strip at the bottom, and you should hear a drum groove with a plucked melody.

Keep the m320 files in this folder: the sound check and the course patches use them.

patching-style.maxpat is the example from the Patching Style Guide on Canvas.

You can also download this folder on its own as a ZIP from the Canvas Software Setup page.
